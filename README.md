# concrete-compaction-methods-study
Experimental study evaluating the effects of manual rodding vs. poker vibration compaction on concrete density, compressive strength, and failure patterns (ASTM C192 / ASTM C39).

#  Effect of Compaction Method on Concrete Density and Strength

**Course:** CV215L – Construction Materials & Concrete Technology | GIK Institute  
**Authors:** Umar Gohar Ali, Muhammad Khan, Saif Ur Rehman  
**Instructor:** Engr. Saad Ilyas | **Date:** December 19, 2025  

---

### Overview & Objectives
An experimental evaluation comparing manual rodding with mechanical poker vibration regarding fresh/hardened density, 7-day compressive strength, and failure patterns in accordance with **ASTM C192** and **ASTM C39**.

* **Mix Proportions:** 1:2:4 (Cement : Sand : Coarse Aggregate) by weight
* **Water/Cement Ratio:** ~0.7 effective
* **Workability:** 40 mm slump (low workability)

---

### Summary of Results

| Metric / Parameter | Standard Rodding (C1, C2) | Poker Vibrator (C3, C4) | Performance Difference |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Avg. Fresh Density** | 2398.60 kg/m³ | 2498.38 kg/m³ | **+4.1%** |
| **Avg. Hardened Density** | 2403.03 kg/m³ | 2515.54 kg/m³ | **+4.7%** |
| **Avg. Failure Load** | 136.02 kN | 146.06 kN | **+7.4%** |
| **Avg. 7-Day Compressive Strength** | **7.70 MPa** | **8.27 MPa** | **+7.4% strength gain** |
| **Strength Variance ($\Delta\sigma$)** | 2.39 MPa (6.50 to 8.89) | 0.11 MPa (8.21 to 8.32) | Significantly more consistent |

---

### Key Findings & Failure Modes

* **Air Void Elimination:** Mechanical vibration reduced entrapped air content from roughly 5–10% down to under 3%, fluidizing the stiff mix far more effectively than manual tamping.
* **Failure Characteristics:**
  * **Manual Rodding:** Visible surface honeycombing and void networks created localized stress concentrations, leading to premature end-crushing and high test variance.
  * **Poker Vibration:** Produced dense, uniform specimens exhibiting clean vertical shear cracking across the cylinder height.

---

### Takeaways & Recommendations

1. **Performance:** Mechanical poker vibration consistently outperforms manual rodding in both density (+4.7%) and compressive strength (+7.4%).
2. **Workability Match:** Vibration is mandatory for low-slump mixes (<50 mm) where standard rodding cannot adequately consolidate internal air pockets.
3. **Field Guidance:** Restrict manual rodding to high-slump or non-structural applications; mandate mechanical vibrators for structural elements to avoid premature structural failure.
